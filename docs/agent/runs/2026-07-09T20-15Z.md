## Decision — advance a genuinely unblocked correctness fix (not gated on #38)
Rather than open a filler PR against the blocked category-aware work, I found a real,
self-contained correctness bug in the already-shipped engine and fixed it.

**Bug:** `recommend_next_card` computes *total first-year value* (owner-confirmed objective,
Decision #1) but subtracted each card's **full listed `annualFee`** — even for cards that
**waive the annual fee the first year** (`isAnnualFeeWaived`). The dataset has **13 cards with a
real fee that's waived year 1** (e.g. Blue Cash Preferred $95, United Explorer $150, Delta Gold
$150). Their true first-year value was understated by $79–$150 each, so a waived-fee card could
be ranked *below* a card that actually costs more in year one. The field was already present in
the dataset (and even in the `NextCardRecommendation` schema as `is_annual_fee_waived`) but the
scoring math never read it. No category data and no human decision needed — fully unblocked.
